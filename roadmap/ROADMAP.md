# Public Roadmap

## D1–D30 — Mobilize
Establish governance, confirm scope/stakeholders, complete kickoff, establish risk controls, and submit the first contractual package.

## D10–D120 — Discover, design, and prototype
Run requirements workshops, define workflows/data requirements, finalize SRS and architecture, design APIs/data/UX, build a critical-flow prototype, define testing, and establish development/UAT foundations.

## D80–D175 — Build in parallel workstreams
Develop platform foundation, integration and OCR, transaction/graph analysis, case management, refund/asset workflows, chatbot, reporting, and dashboard capabilities.

## D150–D210 — Integrate and verify
Execute SIT, remediation, security/performance testing, UAT cycles, regression, and compile Phase 3 evidence.

## D195–D240 — Production readiness and closeout
Prepare production, validate deployment/rollback, finalize manuals, train users/admins, go live, perform early-life support, complete handover, and submit the final report.

## Key internal planning gates
- D60 — Requirement baseline
- D90 — Architecture / prototype baseline
- D120 — Design sign-off
- D175 — Feature complete target
- D195 — SIT exit target
- D205 — UAT sign-off target
- D225 — Go-live readiness target
- D240 — Final acceptance

Internal target gates are project-management controls and do not replace contractual acceptance milestones.
